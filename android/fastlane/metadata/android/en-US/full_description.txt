DolarBlue is the simplest and fastest app to check dollar rates in Argentina.

Main features:
💵 Real-time Dólar Blue and Dólar Oficial rates
💶 Euro Blue and Euro Oficial rates
📊 Percent gap between Blue and Official
🧮 ARS ⇄ USD / EUR calculator
📋 Locally saved conversion history
📤 Share rates via WhatsApp, social media and more
🌙 Dark UI optimized for daily use

Data provided by Bluelytics.com.ar
